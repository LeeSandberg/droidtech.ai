2. What Personal Data We Collect

2.1 When you purchase a license

When you buy the Agentic Embedded Debugger through our website (payments handled by Stripe), we collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Billing address
  • Company name and VAT number (if provided)
  • Purchase details (product, price, date, currency)
  • License key assigned to you

Payment card details are processed directly by Stripe; we do not see or store full card details.

2.2 When we generate and send your license

Our licensing system, hosted on Hetzner servers in Germany, processes:

  • Your email address
  • Your license key
  • Purchase and activation timestamps
  • Technical logs necessary for troubleshooting delivery

We use Resend to send your license key to your email address. Resend processes email address and delivery metadata.

2.3 When you contact our support

When you contact us at support@droidtech.ai, we process:

  • Your email address and name (if provided)
  • Content of your message and attachments
  • Technical information you choose to share (e.g. logs, screenshots)

2.4 Website visits and cookies

Our web hosting and security providers may collect standard server logs (IP address, browser type, date and time, etc.). If we use analytics or other non-essential cookies, we will ask for your consent via a cookie banner. For details, see our Cookie Policy.

3. What We Do Not Collect

Agentic Embedded Debugger is a client-side / customer-controlled tool. It connects to your own AI services and infrastructure. We do not:

  • Collect or store prompts, outputs or other AI-related content processed through your systems
  • Access your internal code, datasets or business-specific data through the product
  • Use customer data from the product to train any models

The MCP software does not send customer content to Droidtech's servers. Our role is limited to licensing, updates, and support.

Optional Data Sharing: You may, at your sole discretion, choose to opt in to share additional data with us for purposes such as product improvement, troubleshooting, or feature development. Any such data sharing would be subject to your explicit prior consent, and you may withdraw such consent at any time. Where you provide consent to share data, we will process it in accordance with the purposes specified at the time of collection and the legal basis of consent under GDPR Article 6(1)(a).
